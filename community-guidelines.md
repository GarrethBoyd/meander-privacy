# Meander — Community Guidelines

[Privacy Policy](https://garrethboyd.github.io/meander-privacy/) · **Community Guidelines** · [User Guide](https://garrethboyd.github.io/meander-privacy/guide/) · [Support](https://garrethboyd.github.io/meander-privacy/support)

**Last updated: 11 November 2026**

Meander's Friends and Groups are for encouraging each other to walk. These
guidelines cover everything you share with friends and group members: your
name, your profile photo, your activity and its walk photos, and the names of
groups you create. By using Friends, you agree to them.

---

## Don't share

- Nudity or sexual content.
- Violence or gore, or anything that threatens or encourages harm.
- Hate: content that attacks people for their race, ethnicity, religion,
  disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or anything similar.
- Harassment or bullying, including names or photos aimed at someone.
- Other people's private information, or photos of people who wouldn't want
  them shared.
- Anything illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights.

**There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour.**

---

## Your controls

- **Show, blur or hide a friend's photos.** Choose what you see of each
  friend's activity photos and profile photo, from their Activity tab or
  **Settings ▸ Friends ▸ Manage Friends**. They aren't told.
- **Unfriend** anyone. You both stop seeing each other's stats.
- **Block** someone to unfriend them and stop them inviting you again.
- **Report** a photo, a name or a group from where you see it.

---

## When you report something

Your report comes to us by email, with what you reported attached. Meander
can't see your friends or what they share, so your report is how we find out.

**We aim to review reports within 48 hours.** We check what was reported against
the list above:

- **If it doesn't break these guidelines**, we'll let you know. You can still
  block the person, or blur or hide their photos.
- **The first time someone breaks them**, what was reported is removed (their
  profile photo, their activity photos, their name or a group's name), and
  they're told why the next time they open Meander. They can carry on using
  Friends.
- **If they break them again**, their account is removed from Friends and
  Groups for everyone. They can still use the rest of Meander.
- **Serious breaches**, such as sexual content involving anyone who appears to
  be under 18, or threats of violence, remove the account from Friends straight
  away, without a warning.

The person you report isn't told who reported them.

If you think your account was removed by mistake, email us.

---

## Changes

If these guidelines change, Meander will ask you to agree to them again before
you next share with friends.
