# Meander — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

Meander is a walking journal. It records your walks, the route you took, and any
photos you choose to capture along the way.

This policy explains what Meander does with that information. The short version:
your walks stay on your device and in your own private iCloud account. The
developer of Meander cannot see any of it. 

---

## What Meander stores, and where

### On your device
- **Walks** — start and end time, distance, duration, and the GPS route
- **Photos** — images you capture during a walk, with the location and time
  they were taken
- **Place names** — the name of the area a walk happened in, looked up from the
  route's coordinates using Apple Maps (see below)
- **Progress** — your level, points, achievements, and walking goals
- **Preferences** — your settings, chosen theme, and notification choices

### In your private iCloud account
If you are signed in to iCloud, your walks, photos, achievements, goals, and
progress sync to the **private database** of your own iCloud account. This is
what lets your history return when you reinstall Meander or move to a new
device.

This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is not visible to the
developer of Meander.

Because it lives in your own iCloud account, **it uses your iCloud storage
allowance.** Walk photos account for nearly all of it — walk records themselves
are only a few kilobytes each. If you run low on iCloud storage you can stop
Meander syncing at any time, or remove its data, using the steps below.

Your preferences and settings are **not** synced — they stay on each device.

To stop syncing, go to **Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Apps Using iCloud**
and switch Meander off. Anything already on your device stays there.

### Nowhere else
Meander has no servers. There is no Meander account to create, no login, and no
database belonging to the developer. Your walks are never uploaded to us,
because there is nowhere to upload them to.

### Apple Maps
To name the place a walk happened, and to suggest nearby places for Nearby
Wanders, Meander sends a location to **Apple's map services** and receives
place names back. This is the same service the Maps app uses. The request goes
to Apple, not to the developer, and is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.
Meander does not send your route history, photos, or any account details with
it.

## Permissions Meander asks for, and why
**Location** — to record the route of a walk, and to look up the name of the
area you walked in. Meander requests background location so tracking continues
while your phone is in your pocket or the screen is off; that background access
is used only while a walk is running.

If **Nearby Wanders** is switched on, Meander also uses your current location
when you open the Walk tab, to suggest scenic places nearby. You can switch
Nearby Wanders off in Meander's Settings.

**Camera** — to capture photos during a walk.

**Photos** — to save walk photos to your Photos library when you ask it to,
keeping each photo's original date and location. Meander Pro also files them in
a "Meander" album. iOS requires full Photos access for an app to create and add
to an album, so that is what Meander asks for — but it only ever **adds** photos.
It never reads, scans, or uploads the rest of your library.

**Apple Health** — with your permission, Meander saves each completed walk to
Apple Health as a walking workout, including its distance, active calories, and
route, so it counts toward your activity rings. To estimate those calories it
reads your **most recent body weight** from Health; the weight itself is not
stored or synced by Meander. Health data is used **only** to show you your own
walking statistics. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never shared
with third parties, and never sent to the developer.

**Notifications** — for walk milestones, achievements, and optional reminders.
These are scheduled on your device; no notification content is sent from a
server.

**Voice announcements** — optional spoken updates during a walk are generated on
your device by iOS. Meander does not use the microphone.

### Lock Screen and widgets
During a walk, a Live Activity can show your distance and time on the Lock
Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Meander's widgets show your walking stats
on your Home Screen. This information stays on your device, but it is visible
to anyone who can see your screen. You can turn Live Activities off, or remove
the widgets, at any time.

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.
Meander continues to work with reduced functionality.

## Analytics and tracking
There are none. Meander contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no
crash reporting services, and no third-party trackers of any kind. Nothing you
do in the app is reported to the developer by Meander itself.

The one exception is controlled by you and by Apple: if you have chosen to
**Share With App Developers** in **Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics &
Improvements**, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and
usage statistics. These do not identify you, and you can switch this off at any
time.

Meander does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not ask for tracking
permission, because it does not track you.

## Purchases
Meander Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription, sold and processed
entirely by Apple through the App Store.

The developer never sees your payment details, card number, or billing address.
Meander only receives confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active.

Manage or cancel in **Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions**.

---

## Children
Meander is not directed at children and collects no personal information about
anyone, including children.

---

## How long data is kept
Everything Meander stores stays until **you** delete it — there is no automatic
expiry, and nothing is kept anywhere after you delete it from the app and from
iCloud. Walks already saved to Apple Health are kept by Health until you delete
them there.

---

## Your rights
Because Meander holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to
disclose, correct, export, or delete on your behalf.

You are in direct control of everything Meander stores:
- **Delete individual walks or photos** inside the app. Deletions sync, so they
  are removed from iCloud too.
- **Delete everything** by deleting the app, and by removing Meander's data
  under **Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage**.
