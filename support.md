## Common questions

**My walks or photos aren't on my new iPhone.**
Meander restores from your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the
same Apple Account, and that iCloud is switched on for Meander in
**Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Apps Using iCloud**. Photos can take a few
minutes to download after walks appear.

**Photos say "will sync later".**
Your walk is saved on your iPhone. Photos upload to iCloud automatically the next
time you have a connection — just open Meander.

**How do I cancel Meander Pro?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: **Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸
Meander**.

**How do I restore Meander Pro on a new device?**
Open the Meander Pro screen and tap **Restore**, using the Apple Account you
subscribed with.

**Walks aren't appearing in Apple Health.**
Check **Settings ▸ Health ▸ Data Access & Devices ▸ Meander** and allow Meander to
write workouts.
